# G0 – English

## Goals

- Target exam score: **70–90%**.
- Make English a low-stress, high-value section.

## Daily checklist

- [ ] 20–30 minutes reading (technical or general).
- [ ] 20 new vocab + spaced repetition review.
- [ ] 5–10 grammar/structure questions.

## Weekly checklist

- [ ] 1 timed mini test (~20–30 questions).
- [ ] Log mistakes into a small error list:
  - [ ] Word choice / collocation errors.
  - [ ] Reading misunderstanding.
  - [ ] Grammar patterns.
