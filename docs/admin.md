# Admin & Registration Guide

This file centralises everything related to **Sanjesh registration**, official documents, and bureaucratic constraints.

## 1. Timeline

- Registration window: around **7–14 Dec 2025** (16–23 Azar 1404) – always re-check on `sanjesh.org`.
- Exam days: **7–8 May 2026** (17–18 Ordibehesht 1405).

## 2. Registration checklist

- [ ] Read the latest official brochure (دفترچه ارشد ۱۴۰۵).
- [ ] Confirm you meet the **eligibility criteria** (degree, GPA, etc.).
- [ ] Prepare and verify digital photo.
- [ ] Pay the fee and store the **payment serial**.
- [ ] Complete online form accurately.
- [ ] Save **confirmation page**, serial, and tracking number as PDF.

## 3. Identity & education details

- [ ] National ID number
- [ ] Birth certificate series & number
- [ ] Full legal name (in Persian, as requested)
- [ ] Bachelor’s university, major, entry year, graduation (or expected) year
- [ ] GPA (final or partial form for last-year students)

## 4. Military service (if applicable)

- [ ] Make sure your **status code** is valid for exam participation and for future enrolment.
- [ ] If in doubt, talk to your university education office or relevant authorities **early**, not last minute.

## 5. After registration

- [ ] Note your **exam field code** (Computer Engineering – 1277).
- [ ] Track any **Sanjesh news** related to exam date/location changes.
- [ ] Download and double-check admission card when released.
