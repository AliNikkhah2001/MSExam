# G1 – Math Pack (Engineering, Diff Eq, Prob/Stats, Discrete)

## Target

- Overall: **40–60%** in the exam.

## Structure

- [ ] Engineering Math & Differential Equations.
- [ ] Probability & Statistics.
- [ ] Discrete Mathematics.

## Subtasks

### Engineering Math & Diff Eq

- [ ] Review core calculus (limits, derivatives, integrals).
- [ ] Linear algebra basics (vectors, matrices, eigenvalues).
- [ ] Differential equations (1st and 2nd order, basic systems).
- [ ] 100–150 targeted exam-style questions.

### Probability & Statistics

- [ ] Definitions (sample space, events, RVs).
- [ ] Discrete and continuous distributions.
- [ ] Expectation, variance, covariance.
- [ ] Conditional probability & Bayes.
- [ ] 100–150 questions.

### Discrete Math

- [ ] Sets, relations, functions.
- [ ] Combinatorics and counting.
- [ ] Recurrence relations.
- [ ] Graphs & trees basics.
- [ ] 100–150 questions.
